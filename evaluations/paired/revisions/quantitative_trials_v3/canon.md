# Investment research memo

As of 2022-04-27T12:00:00Z

Forecast direction: within

**Research question**
Using Canon’s first quarter FY2022 release, what consolidated net income attributable to Canon Inc. remained to be earned to meet its December year-end projection? Give your call on the reported annual result relative to the projection (within means ±2% of guidance, inclusive). State one base-case direction (lower, within, or higher), the main driver, an alternative and an observable signal that would change the call. Cite PDF pages for material claims and show the inputs to derived figures.

## Answer

Canon needed 206,025 in consolidated net income attributable to Canon Inc. over the remaining three quarters: JPY 252,000 million projected for the year ending in December minus JPY 45,975 million earned in the quarter ended in March. Both figures exclude income attributable to noncontrolling interests. [1, PDF p1 §1](https://global.canon/en/ir/results/2022/rslt2022q1e.pdf#page=1&search=CONSOLIDATED%20RESULTS%20FOR%20THE%20FIRST%20QUARTER) [2, PDF p7 §1](https://global.canon/en/ir/results/2022/rslt2022q1e.pdf#page=7&search=CANON%20INC.%20AND%20SUBSIDIARIES%20CONSOLIDATED%202.)

The question’s ±2% tolerance applies to full-year guidance, not the amount remaining. Multiplying JPY 252,000 million by one minus the tolerance gives the lower bound of 246,960; multiplying it by one plus the tolerance gives the upper bound of 257,040. Both endpoints count as within. [1, PDF p1 §1](https://global.canon/en/ir/results/2022/rslt2022q1e.pdf#page=1&search=CONSOLIDATED%20RESULTS%20FOR%20THE%20FIRST%20QUARTER)

## Outlook

Base case as of 2022-04-27T12:00:00Z: within. The main driver is Canon’s ability to ship printers into recovering office demand. Canon had already switched parts and added suppliers to sustain production. Its upward revision incorporated those measures and assumed exchange rates of ¥120 to the dollar and ¥130 to the euro. Moderate relief from chip shortages and freight disruption in the second half would help Canon fill orders. That supports the revised projection rather than providing an additional reason to expect a beat. [3, PDF p2 §2](https://global.canon/en/ir/results/2022/rslt2022q1e.pdf#page=2&search=production%20delays%20%7C%20due%20to%20the) [4, PDF p4 §3](https://global.canon/en/ir/results/2022/rslt2022q1e.pdf#page=4&search=devices%20including%20laptops%20and%20tablets.%20With) [5, PDF p4 §2](https://global.canon/en/ir/results/2022/rslt2022q1e.pdf#page=4&search=and%20region.%20In%20the%20markets%20in)

## What would change the view

The strongest alternative is lower. First-quarter attributable income grew 3.4% year on year against projected full-year growth of 17.4%. Office multifunction-device demand recovered, yet chip shortages kept unit sales below the prior year. I would switch to lower if Canon still reports chip-driven year-on-year unit declines for those devices in the second half despite recovering demand. That would undercut the expected supply recovery; the first-quarter release alone cannot establish its timing or earnings impact.
