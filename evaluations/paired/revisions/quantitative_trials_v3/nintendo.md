# Investment research memo

As of 2020-05-08T12:00:00Z

Forecast direction: higher

**Research question**
At Nintendo’s FY2020 results, what profit attributable to owners of parent did it guide for the year ending March 2021? Compare it with the just-reported year, discuss the risk to the supply of components, and forecast the full-year actual relative to guidance (within means ±2% of the guide, inclusive). State one base-case direction (lower, within, or higher), the main driver, an alternative and an observable signal that would change the call. Cite PDF pages for material claims and show the inputs to derived figures.

## Answer

Nintendo initially guided to JPY 200,000 million in consolidated profit attributable to owners of parent for the year ending March 31, 2021, versus JPY 258,641 million for the year ended March 31, 2020. The change was JPY (200,000 − 258,641) million = JPY -58,641 million. Dividing that change by the prior year's JPY 258,641 million and expressing the result as a percentage gives -22.6727. Nintendo could therefore beat guidance even if profit fell year over year. [1, PDF p2 §1](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ir/pdf/2020/200507e.pdf#page=2&search=Consolidated%20Financial%20Forecast%20for%20the%20Fiscal) [2, PDF p1 §1](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ir/pdf/2020/200507e.pdf#page=1&search=CONSOLIDATED%20FINANCIAL%20STATEMENTS%20May%207%2C%202020)

## Evidence

Nintendo forecast 14,000 ten-thousand units of Switch software, against 16,872 on the same scale in the just-reported year. Actual sales included copies bundled with hardware; the forecast excluded them. The reported gap is therefore not a like-for-like measure of how far demand could fall before sales missed the plan. [3, PDF p14 §1](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ir/pdf/2020/200507e.pdf#page=14&search=%284%29%20Consolidated%20sales%20units%2C%20number%20of)

The stronger case for upside is that demand extended beyond a single launch. Animal Crossing: New Horizons sold 11.77 million units after its March release, while popular older titles continued to grow and downloadable versions of packaged games sold well. Downloads gave existing owners a way to keep buying even when stores closed. The March sales belonged to the prior year: they support continued demand but do not establish its duration or its contribution to full-year profit. [4, PDF p3 §1](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ir/pdf/2020/200507e.pdf#page=3&search=Description%20of%20Operating%20Results%201.%20Operating) [5, PDF p3 §2](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ir/pdf/2020/200507e.pdf#page=3&search=year%20totaled%2021.03%20million%20units%20%2824.0%25) [6, PDF p4 §1](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ir/pdf/2020/200507e.pdf#page=4&search=%E3%83%BB%20Impact%20on%20consumption%20activities%20There)

## Outlook

Higher is my base case as of 2020-05-08T12:00:00Z. Continued game purchases by existing Switch owners could lift software sales above plan without requiring stronger console shipments. Applying the question's ±2% tolerance to JPY 200,000 million, the lower multiplier is one minus two percent and the upper multiplier is one plus two percent. The resulting within-guidance range is JPY 196,000 million to JPY 204,000 million, inclusive; the call is for profit strictly above the upper bound. [1, PDF p2 §1](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ir/pdf/2020/200507e.pdf#page=2&search=Consolidated%20Financial%20Forecast%20for%20the%20Fiscal) [3, PDF p14 §1](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ir/pdf/2020/200507e.pdf#page=14&search=%284%29%20Consolidated%20sales%20units%2C%20number%20of) [4, PDF p3 §1](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ir/pdf/2020/200507e.pdf#page=3&search=Description%20of%20Operating%20Results%201.%20Operating)

## What would change the view

Lower is the strongest alternative. Production and shipping delays appeared to be easing, but Nintendo warned that continued difficulty procuring components could disrupt supply. Persistent shortages could constrain Switch assembly, cost console sales and leave fewer new owners buying games. The guide already assumed Nintendo could meet full-year demand and release software as planned, so supply recovery alone would not justify a beat. I would switch to lower if Nintendo reported component-related shipment shortfalls heading into the holiday season and indicated it could not recover the lost volume by fiscal year-end. Its disclosures did not quantify that potential profit loss. [7, PDF p3 §4](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ir/pdf/2020/200507e.pdf#page=3&search=Net%20cash%20provided%20by%20%28used%20in%29) [8, PDF p4 §3](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ir/pdf/2020/200507e.pdf#page=4&search=forecasting%20are%20105%20yen%20per%20US)
