# Continuation of a truncated masked assessment

After all 48 original assignments were locked and before opening this study’s forecast outcomes, one masked grader reached its provider output limit. The provider returned an incomplete response with 4,096 billed output tokens. Its confirmed cost was USD 0.208272. It produced no usable grade.

For any such confirmed truncation in this comparison, reproduce the original request fingerprint and continue the same rubric, masked research report, dated sources, model, effort and response schema with an 8,192-token output allowance. The original and continuation together have a USD 1.00 ceiling. The original USD 0.50 grading record and charge remain intact. Both attempts count toward total evaluation cost. An unresolved response, a completed assessment or a low score cannot trigger this rule. The researcher never sees the grading material.

The original score validator still requires verbatim witnesses and complete coverage of the report. A correction for invalid evidence uses the original validation procedure. The continuation ends with an offline-replayable, independently checked receipt and an assessment fingerprint. These charges affect evaluation cost only. Each research arm retains its original USD 0.30 spending limit and its original reported score.
