# NVIDIA’s second-quarter revenue outlook

**As of 2023-05-25**

As of 25 May 2023, would NVIDIA’s total revenue for Q2 FY2024 finish below, within or above its own guidance range? Pick a base case, explain the driver, give a plausible alternative and identify an observable signal before the earnings release that would change your view.

## Known at the cutoff

As of May 25, 2023, NVIDIA’s management guidance for Q2 FY2024 total revenue was USD 11.00 billion, plus or minus 2%. Management reported surging demand and said it was increasing supply. [NVIDIA FY2024 Q1 CFO commentary, Second-quarter guidance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581023000087/q1fy24cfocommentary.htm#:~:text=Second%20Quarter%20of%20Fiscal%202024%20Outlook%20Outlook%20for%20the%20second%20quarter%20of%20fiscal%202024%20is) [NVIDIA FY2024 Q1 earnings release, Demand and production](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581023000087/q1fy24pr.htm#:~:text=%E2%80%9CThe%20computer%20industry%20is%20going%20through%20two%20simultaneous%20transitions%20%E2%80%94%20accelerated%20computing%20and%20generative%20AI%2C%E2%80%9D)

## Base case (within)

I expect Q2 FY2024 total revenue to finish within guidance. The new outlook likely already accounts for strong AI demand and the planned supply ramp. Demand alone does not show that NVIDIA can ship more GPUs within the quarter than management expected. [NVIDIA FY2024 Q1 earnings release, Demand and production](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581023000087/q1fy24pr.htm#:~:text=%E2%80%9CThe%20computer%20industry%20is%20going%20through%20two%20simultaneous%20transitions%20%E2%80%94%20accelerated%20computing%20and%20generative%20AI%2C%E2%80%9D) [NVIDIA FY2024 Q1 CFO commentary, Revenue and Data Center demand](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581023000087/q1fy24cfocommentary.htm#:~:text=Revenue%20was%20%247.19%20billion%2C%20down%2013%25%20from%20a%20year%20ago%20and%20up%2019%25%20sequentially.%20Data)

## Alternative

Above guidance is plausible if NVIDIA ships more Hopper GPUs during the quarter than management planned. Strong demand from cloud providers and large internet companies makes it likely that extra supply would find buyers. [NVIDIA FY2024 Q1 CFO commentary, Revenue and Data Center demand](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581023000087/q1fy24cfocommentary.htm#:~:text=Revenue%20was%20%247.19%20billion%2C%20down%2013%25%20from%20a%20year%20ago%20and%20up%2019%25%20sequentially.%20Data) [NVIDIA FY2024 Q1 earnings release, Demand and production](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581023000087/q1fy24pr.htm#:~:text=%E2%80%9CThe%20computer%20industry%20is%20going%20through%20two%20simultaneous%20transitions%20%E2%80%94%20accelerated%20computing%20and%20generative%20AI%2C%E2%80%9D)

## What would change the view

Before earnings, I would shift toward above guidance if major cloud providers confirm that H100 instances became broadly available earlier than expected during Q2. That would suggest faster deliveries; NVIDIA’s production announcement did not establish shipment timing.
