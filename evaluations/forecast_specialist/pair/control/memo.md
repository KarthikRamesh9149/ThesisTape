# NVIDIA’s second-quarter revenue outlook

**As of 2023-05-25**

As of 25 May 2023, would NVIDIA’s total revenue for Q2 FY2024 finish below, within or above its own guidance range? Pick a base case, explain the driver, give a plausible alternative and identify an observable signal before the earnings release that would change your view.

## Known at the cutoff

On May 24, 2023, NVIDIA guided to Q2 FY2024 total revenue of USD 11.00 billion, plus or minus 2%. Management said it was increasing supply to meet surging demand. [NVIDIA FY2024 Q1 CFO commentary, Second-quarter guidance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581023000087/q1fy24cfocommentary.htm#:~:text=Second%20Quarter%20of%20Fiscal%202024%20Outlook%20Outlook%20for%20the%20second%20quarter%20of%20fiscal%202024%20is) [NVIDIA FY2024 Q1 earnings release, Demand and production](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581023000087/q1fy24pr.htm#:~:text=%E2%80%9CThe%20computer%20industry%20is%20going%20through%20two%20simultaneous%20transitions%20%E2%80%94%20accelerated%20computing%20and%20generative%20AI%2C%E2%80%9D)

## Base case (within)

As of May 25, my base case is that Q2 FY2024 total revenue finishes within guidance. The new outlook likely already accounts for strong AI demand and the planned supply ramp; strong demand alone is not a reason to expect a beat. [NVIDIA FY2024 Q1 CFO commentary, Revenue and Data Center demand](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581023000087/q1fy24cfocommentary.htm#:~:text=Revenue%20was%20%247.19%20billion%2C%20down%2013%25%20from%20a%20year%20ago%20and%20up%2019%25%20sequentially.%20Data) [NVIDIA FY2024 Q1 earnings release, Demand and production](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581023000087/q1fy24pr.htm#:~:text=%E2%80%9CThe%20computer%20industry%20is%20going%20through%20two%20simultaneous%20transitions%20%E2%80%94%20accelerated%20computing%20and%20generative%20AI%2C%E2%80%9D)

## Alternative

Above guidance is plausible if NVIDIA ships more Hopper GPUs than planned and books the extra sales in Q2. Management reported that H100 was in production, but did not establish when shipments would reach customers. [NVIDIA FY2024 Q1 earnings release, Demand and production](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581023000087/q1fy24pr.htm#:~:text=%E2%80%9CThe%20computer%20industry%20is%20going%20through%20two%20simultaneous%20transitions%20%E2%80%94%20accelerated%20computing%20and%20generative%20AI%2C%E2%80%9D) [NVIDIA FY2024 Q1 CFO commentary, Revenue and Data Center demand](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581023000087/q1fy24cfocommentary.htm#:~:text=Revenue%20was%20%247.19%20billion%2C%20down%2013%25%20from%20a%20year%20ago%20and%20up%2019%25%20sequentially.%20Data)

## What would change the view

Before earnings, I would tilt toward above guidance if major cloud providers confirm broad availability of H100 instances earlier than expected. Broad access, rather than a limited preview, would suggest faster shipments, though it would not prove that NVIDIA booked the sales in Q2.
