# NVIDIA’s second-quarter revenue outlook

**As of 2023-05-25**

As of 25 May 2023, would NVIDIA’s total revenue for Q2 FY2024 finish below, within or above its own guidance range? Pick a base case, explain the driver, give a plausible alternative and identify an observable signal before the earnings release that would change your view.

## Known at the cutoff

On 24 May 2023, NVIDIA guided to Q2 FY2024 revenue of USD 11.00 billion, plus or minus 2%. It reported Q1 revenue of USD 7.19 billion, up 19% sequentially, so the outlook called for a substantial step-up. [NVIDIA FY2024 Q1 CFO commentary, Second-quarter guidance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581023000087/q1fy24cfocommentary.htm#:~:text=Second%20Quarter%20of%20Fiscal%202024%20Outlook%20Outlook%20for%20the%20second%20quarter%20of%20fiscal%202024%20is) [NVIDIA FY2024 Q1 CFO commentary, Revenue and Data Center demand](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581023000087/q1fy24cfocommentary.htm#:~:text=Revenue%20was%20%247.19%20billion%2C%20down%2013%25%20from%20a%20year%20ago%20and%20up%2019%25%20sequentially.%20Data)

## Base case (within)

As of 25 May 2023, I expect Q2 FY2024 total revenue to finish within guidance. Strong cloud and internet-company demand was already lifting Data Center revenue, and I expect the announced supply ramp to turn enough of that demand into shipments and recognized revenue in Q2. The outlook already appears to account for the ramp, so strong demand alone is not a reason to call a beat. [NVIDIA FY2024 Q1 CFO commentary, Revenue and Data Center demand](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581023000087/q1fy24cfocommentary.htm#:~:text=Revenue%20was%20%247.19%20billion%2C%20down%2013%25%20from%20a%20year%20ago%20and%20up%2019%25%20sequentially.%20Data) [NVIDIA FY2024 Q1 earnings release, Demand and production](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581023000087/q1fy24pr.htm#:~:text=%E2%80%9CThe%20computer%20industry%20is%20going%20through%20two%20simultaneous%20transitions%20%E2%80%94%20accelerated%20computing%20and%20generative%20AI%2C%E2%80%9D)

## Alternative

An above-range result is plausible if NVIDIA ships more Hopper GPUs than its outlook assumes and recognizes the additional revenue in Q2. Management reported rising supply and surging demand, but did not establish how much would ship by quarter-end. [NVIDIA FY2024 Q1 earnings release, Demand and production](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581023000087/q1fy24pr.htm#:~:text=%E2%80%9CThe%20computer%20industry%20is%20going%20through%20two%20simultaneous%20transitions%20%E2%80%94%20accelerated%20computing%20and%20generative%20AI%2C%E2%80%9D) [NVIDIA FY2024 Q1 CFO commentary, Revenue and Data Center demand](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581023000087/q1fy24cfocommentary.htm#:~:text=Revenue%20was%20%247.19%20billion%2C%20down%2013%25%20from%20a%20year%20ago%20and%20up%2019%25%20sequentially.%20Data)

## What would change the view

Before earnings, I would move toward an above-range forecast if major cloud providers confirmed broad H100 instance availability ahead of their announced schedules, backed by more GPUs delivered by quarter-end than planned. An earlier launch alone would not establish higher NVIDIA shipments.
