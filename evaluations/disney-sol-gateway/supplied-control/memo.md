# Disney and Hulu

**As of 2023-11-02**

As of 2 November 2023, what did Disney expect to pay for NBCUniversal’s stake in Hulu, and what could change the final amount?

## Expected initial payment

As of 2 November 2023, Disney expected to pay NBCUniversal approximately USD 8.61 billion on or before 1 December 2023, assuming the full USD 567.0 million in outstanding capital call contributions remained unpaid on the payment date. Those unpaid contributions would be deducted from the approximately USD 9.17 billion guaranteed purchase price. [Disney 8-K, P1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=On%20or%20before%20December%201%2C%202023%2C%20Disney%20is%20required%20to%20pay%20NBCU%20the%20guaranteed%20purchase%20price) [Disney 8-K, P1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=Disney%20expects%20it%20will%20pay%20NBCU%20approximately%20%248.61%20billion)

## Valuation basis

NBCUniversal exercised its right to require Disney to buy its Hulu stake. The price was based on NBCUniversal’s equity ownership percentage, disclosed as 33% subject to dilution under certain circumstances, applied to the greater of Hulu’s equity fair value and the USD 27.5 billion guaranteed equity value floor. [Disney 8-K, P1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=which%20is%20owned%2067%25%20by%20Disney%20and%2033%25%20by%20NBCU%2C%20subject%20to%20dilution%20under%20certain%20circumstances) [Disney 8-K, P1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=the%20greater%20of%20Hulu%E2%80%99s%20equity%20fair%20value%20or%20a%20guaranteed%20floor%20value%20of%20%2427.5%20billion)

## Appraisal and outstanding uncertainty

Hulu’s equity fair value as of 30 September 2023 remained subject to the agreed appraisal process. If the final appraisal exceeded the floor, Disney would pay NBCUniversal its ownership share of that appraised value less the guaranteed purchase price within 45 days of the final determination. Separately, Disney would share 50% of its estimated U.S. tax savings from the acquisition with NBCUniversal, with those payments expected primarily over 15 years. [Disney 8-K, P2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=Disney%20will%20share%20with%20NBCU%2050%25%20of%20Disney%E2%80%99s%20estimated%20U.S.%20tax%20savings%20resulting%20from%20the%20acquisition)

## Arithmetic check

- Guaranteed purchase price **USD 9,170,000,000**. [Disclosed gross](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=approximately%20%249.17%20billion%20%28the%20%E2%80%9Cguaranteed%20purchase%20price%E2%80%9D%29)
- Unpaid capital contributions **USD 567,000,000**. [Disclosed deduction](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=payable%20to%20Disney%2C%20currently%20approximately%20%24567%20million)
- Subtracting the two rounded inputs gives **USD 8,603,000,000**.
- Disney's reported estimate was **USD 8,610,000,000**. [Disclosed estimate](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=Disney%20expects%20it%20will%20pay%20NBCU%20approximately%20%248.61%20billion)

The **USD 7,000,000** difference fits within the precision of the published figures. The reported estimate is the figure used in this memo.
