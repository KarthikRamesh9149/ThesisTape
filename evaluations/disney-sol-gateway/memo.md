# Disney and Hulu

**As of 2023-11-02**

As of 2 November 2023, what did Disney expect to pay for NBCUniversal’s stake in Hulu, and what could change the final amount?

## Expected initial payment

As of November 2, 2023, Disney expected to pay NBCUniversal approximately USD 8.61 billion on or before December 1, 2023, assuming the full approximately USD 567 million in outstanding capital call contributions remained unpaid on the payment date. Any reduction in that unpaid balance would increase the initial payment. [Disney Form 8-K, Item 8.01 Other Events. ¶1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=On%20November%201%2C%202023%2C%20a%20subsidiary%20of%20NBC%20Universal%20%28collectively%20with%20its%20subsidiaries%2C%20%E2%80%9CNBCU%E2%80%9D%29%20provided)

## Valuation basis

The agreement valued NBCUniversal’s stake at its ownership share of the greater of Hulu’s equity fair value or the USD 27.5 billion guaranteed floor for all of Hulu’s equity. NBCUniversal owned 33%, subject to dilution under certain circumstances. Its share of the floor yielded the gross guaranteed purchase price of approximately USD 9.17 billion. [Disney Form 8-K, Item 8.01 Other Events. ¶1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=On%20November%201%2C%202023%2C%20a%20subsidiary%20of%20NBC%20Universal%20%28collectively%20with%20its%20subsidiaries%2C%20%E2%80%9CNBCU%E2%80%9D%29%20provided)

## Appraisal and outstanding uncertainty

The final purchase price was not known as of November 2, 2023. The agreed appraisal would determine Hulu’s equity fair value as of September 30, 2023. If that value exceeded the floor, Disney would pay NBCUniversal its ownership share of the appraised value less the gross guaranteed purchase price within 45 days of the final determination. Separately, Disney would share 50% of its estimated U.S. tax savings from the acquisition, with payments expected primarily over 15 years. The filing did not quantify those payments. [Disney Form 8-K, Item 8.01 Other Events. ¶2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=Hulu%E2%80%99s%20equity%20fair%20value%20will%20be%20assessed%20as%20of%20September%2030%2C%202023%2C%20and%20determined%20in)

## Arithmetic check

- Guaranteed purchase price **USD 9,170,000,000**. [Disclosed gross](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=On%20November%201%2C%202023%2C%20a%20subsidiary%20of%20NBC%20Universal%20%28collectively%20with%20its%20subsidiaries%2C%20%E2%80%9CNBCU%E2%80%9D%29%20provided)
- Unpaid capital contributions **USD 567,000,000**. [Disclosed deduction](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=On%20November%201%2C%202023%2C%20a%20subsidiary%20of%20NBC%20Universal%20%28collectively%20with%20its%20subsidiaries%2C%20%E2%80%9CNBCU%E2%80%9D%29%20provided)
- Subtracting the two rounded inputs gives **USD 8,603,000,000**.
- Disney's reported estimate was **USD 8,610,000,000**. [Disclosed estimate](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=On%20November%201%2C%202023%2C%20a%20subsidiary%20of%20NBC%20Universal%20%28collectively%20with%20its%20subsidiaries%2C%20%E2%80%9CNBCU%E2%80%9D%29%20provided)

The **USD 7,000,000** difference fits within the precision of the published figures. The reported estimate is the figure used in this memo.
