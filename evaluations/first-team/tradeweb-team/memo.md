# Tradeweb adjusted expenses outlook

**As of 2023-10-26T13:17:00Z**

What did Tradeweb disclose about the high end of its forecast range for adjusted expenses?

In its October 26, 2023 Form 8-K/A, Tradeweb corrected a typo in its adjusted expense forecast. The high end of the range was USD 695 million, not the previously reported USD 659 million.
