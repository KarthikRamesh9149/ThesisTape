# Disney and Hulu

**As of 2023-11-02**

As of 2 November 2023, what did Disney expect to pay for NBCUniversal’s stake in Hulu, and what could change the final amount?

## Expected initial payment

As of November 2, 2023, Disney expected to pay NBCUniversal about USD 8.61 billion by December 1, 2023. That was the guaranteed purchase price of about USD 9.17 billion less about USD 567.0 million in capital calls, assuming the full balance remained unpaid on the payment date. A smaller unpaid balance would increase the initial payment. [Disney Form 8-K, Item 8.01 Other Events. ¶1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=On%20November%201%2C%202023%2C%20a%20subsidiary%20of%20NBC%20Universal%20%28collectively%20with%20its%20subsidiaries%2C%20%E2%80%9CNBCU%E2%80%9D%29%20provided)

## Valuation basis

The purchase price was based on NBCUniversal’s ownership percentage of the greater of Hulu’s equity fair value or the USD 27.5 billion guaranteed floor for Hulu as a whole. Separately, Disney agreed to share 50% of its estimated U.S. tax savings from the acquisition, with payments expected primarily over 15 years. [Disney Form 8-K, Item 8.01 Other Events. ¶1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=On%20November%201%2C%202023%2C%20a%20subsidiary%20of%20NBC%20Universal%20%28collectively%20with%20its%20subsidiaries%2C%20%E2%80%9CNBCU%E2%80%9D%29%20provided) [Disney Form 8-K, Item 8.01 Other Events. ¶2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=Hulu%E2%80%99s%20equity%20fair%20value%20will%20be%20assessed%20as%20of%20September%2030%2C%202023%2C%20and%20determined%20in)

## Appraisal and outstanding uncertainty

The final purchase price was not known as of November 2. The agreed appraisal would assess Hulu’s equity fair value as of September 30, 2023. If that value exceeded the floor, Disney would pay NBCUniversal its share of the excess within 45 days of the final determination. [Disney Form 8-K, Item 8.01 Other Events. ¶2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=Hulu%E2%80%99s%20equity%20fair%20value%20will%20be%20assessed%20as%20of%20September%2030%2C%202023%2C%20and%20determined%20in)

## Arithmetic check

- Guaranteed purchase price **USD 9,170,000,000**. [Disclosed gross](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=On%20November%201%2C%202023%2C%20a%20subsidiary%20of%20NBC%20Universal%20%28collectively%20with%20its%20subsidiaries%2C%20%E2%80%9CNBCU%E2%80%9D%29%20provided)
- Unpaid capital contributions **USD 567,000,000**. [Disclosed deduction](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=On%20November%201%2C%202023%2C%20a%20subsidiary%20of%20NBC%20Universal%20%28collectively%20with%20its%20subsidiaries%2C%20%E2%80%9CNBCU%E2%80%9D%29%20provided)
- Subtracting the two rounded inputs gives **USD 8,603,000,000**.
- Disney's reported estimate was **USD 8,610,000,000**. [Disclosed estimate](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=On%20November%201%2C%202023%2C%20a%20subsidiary%20of%20NBC%20Universal%20%28collectively%20with%20its%20subsidiaries%2C%20%E2%80%9CNBCU%E2%80%9D%29%20provided)

The **USD 7,000,000** difference fits within the precision of the published figures. The reported estimate is the figure used in this memo.
