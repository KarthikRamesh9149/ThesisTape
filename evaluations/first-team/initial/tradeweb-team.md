# Tradeweb adjusted expenses outlook

**As of 2023-10-26T13:17:00Z**

What did Tradeweb disclose about the high end of its forecast range for adjusted expenses?

In its October 26, 2023 amended filing, Tradeweb said the high end of its adjusted expense forecast was USD 695 million, not the USD 659 million originally reported. The amendment corrected a typographical error; the rest of the earnings release was unchanged. [Tradeweb Markets Form 8-K/A, Item 2.02.
