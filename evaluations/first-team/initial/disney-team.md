# Disney and Hulu

**As of 2023-11-02**

As of 2 November 2023, what did Disney expect to pay for NBCUniversal’s stake in Hulu, and what could change the final amount?

## Expected initial payment

As of 2 November 2023, Disney expected to pay NBCUniversal approximately USD 8.61 billion on or before 1 December 2023 if all of NBCUniversal’s approximately USD 567.0 million in outstanding capital contributions remained unpaid. Only the amount still unpaid at payment would be deducted. [Disney Form 8-K, Item 8.01 Other Events. ¶1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=On%20November%201%2C%202023%2C%20a%20subsidiary%20of%20NBC%20Universal%20%28collectively%20with%20its%20subsidiaries%2C%20%E2%80%9CNBCU%E2%80%9D%29%20provided)

## Valuation basis

The purchase price was based on NBCUniversal’s equity stake, reported as 33% and subject to dilution, applied to the greater of Hulu’s equity fair value or the guaranteed floor of USD 27.5 billion. Separately, Disney would share 50% of its estimated U.S. tax savings from the acquisition with NBCUniversal, with payments expected primarily over 15 years. [Disney Form 8-K, Item 8.01 Other Events. ¶1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=On%20November%201%2C%202023%2C%20a%20subsidiary%20of%20NBC%20Universal%20%28collectively%20with%20its%20subsidiaries%2C%20%E2%80%9CNBCU%E2%80%9D%29%20provided) [Disney Form 8-K, Item 8.01 Other Events. ¶2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=Hulu%E2%80%99s%20equity%20fair%20value%20will%20be%20assessed%20as%20of%20September%2030%2C%202023%2C%20and%20determined%20in)

## Appraisal and outstanding uncertainty

The final purchase price was not known as of 2 November 2023. The agreed appraisal process would determine Hulu’s equity fair value as of 30 September 2023. If that value exceeded the floor, Disney would pay the difference between NBCUniversal’s share of the appraised value and the gross guaranteed purchase price within 45 days of the final determination. [Disney Form 8-K, Item 8.01 Other Events. ¶2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=Hulu%E2%80%99s%20equity%20fair%20value%20will%20be%20assessed%20as%20of%20September%2030%2C%202023%2C%20and%20determined%20in)

## Arithmetic check

- Guaranteed purchase price **USD 9,170,000,000**. [Disclosed gross](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=On%20November%201%2C%202023%2C%20a%20subsidiary%20of%20NBC%20Universal%20%28collectively%20with%20its%20subsidiaries%2C%20%E2%80%9CNBCU%E2%80%9D%29%20provided)
- Unpaid capital contributions **USD 567,000,000**. [Disclosed deduction](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=On%20November%201%2C%202023%2C%20a%20subsidiary%20of%20NBC%20Universal%20%28collectively%20with%20its%20subsidiaries%2C%20%E2%80%9CNBCU%E2%80%9D%29%20provided)
- Subtracting the two rounded inputs gives **USD 8,603,000,000**.
- Disney's reported estimate was **USD 8,610,000,000**. [Disclosed estimate](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448923000206/dis-20231101.htm#:~:text=On%20November%201%2C%202023%2C%20a%20subsidiary%20of%20NBC%20Universal%20%28collectively%20with%20its%20subsidiaries%2C%20%E2%80%9CNBCU%E2%80%9D%29%20provided)

The **USD 7,000,000** difference fits within the precision of the published figures. The reported estimate is the figure used in this memo.
