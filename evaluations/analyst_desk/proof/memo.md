# Analyst research

**As of 2023-10-27T00:15:00Z**

What did Tradeweb correct about the high end of its 2023 adjusted expense forecast, and what stayed unchanged in the amended filing?

Tradeweb corrected a typographical error in its Adjusted Expenses forecast, raising the high end from $659 million to $695 million. The low end and all other information in the original Form 8-K and earnings release remained unchanged. The excerpts do not specify the period covered by the forecast.
