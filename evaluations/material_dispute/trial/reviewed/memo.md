# Twitter merger status

**As of 2022-07-11T21:00:00Z**

As of 11 July 2022, could a deal analyst treat the Musk buyer group’s termination notice as ending the Twitter acquisition? Give each side’s position, the per-share price and remedy at issue, and what could be concluded about closing.

No. As of 11 July 2022, the notice showed that the Musk buyer group had exercised what it claimed was a right to terminate, not that the acquisition had ended. Musk’s counsel alleged that Twitter materially breached the agreement by failing to provide requested data on fake or spam accounts, appeared to have made misleading representations, and was likely to suffer a Company Material Adverse Effect. The notice invoked Section 8.1(d)(i). [Letter from counsel for Elon Musk and X Holdings I, Grounds asserted in the notice](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1418091/000110465922078413/tm2220599d1_ex99-p.htm#:~:text=We%20refer%20to%20%28i%29%20the%20Agreement%20and%20Plan%20of%20Merger%20by%20and%20among%20X%20Holdings) [Letter from counsel for Elon Musk and X Holdings I, Formal notice](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1418091/000110465922078413/tm2220599d1_ex99-p.htm#:~:text=Accordingly%2C%20for%20all%20of%20these%20reasons%2C%20Mr.%20Musk%20hereby%20exercises%20X%20Holdings%20I%2C%20Inc.%E2%80%99s%20right)

Twitter called the notice invalid, denied breaching the agreement or suffering or being likely to suffer a Company Material Adverse Effect, and maintained that the agreement remained in force. The board said it intended to close at USD 54.20 per share, and Twitter reserved the right to seek specific performance to compel the buyers to fulfill their obligations. The supplied record established neither an effective termination nor a guaranteed closing.
