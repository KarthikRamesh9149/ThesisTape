# Twitter merger status

**As of 2022-07-11T21:00:00Z**

As of 11 July 2022, could a deal analyst treat the Musk buyer group’s termination notice as ending the Twitter acquisition? Give each side’s position, the per-share price and remedy at issue, and what could be concluded about closing.

No. As of 11 July 2022, a deal analyst could treat the notice as an attempt to terminate the merger, not as proof that the acquisition was over. The Musk buyer group invoked its contractual termination right, alleging material breaches, including a failure to provide requested data on fake or spam accounts, apparent misrepresentations, and a likely Company Material Adverse Effect. [Letter from counsel for Elon Musk and X Holdings I, Grounds asserted in the notice](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1418091/000110465922078413/tm2220599d1_ex99-p.htm#:~:text=We%20refer%20to%20%28i%29%20the%20Agreement%20and%20Plan%20of%20Merger%20by%20and%20among%20X%20Holdings) [Letter from counsel for Elon Musk and X Holdings I, Formal notice](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1418091/000110465922078413/tm2220599d1_ex99-p.htm#:~:text=Accordingly%2C%20for%20all%20of%20these%20reasons%2C%20Mr.%20Musk%20hereby%20exercises%20X%20Holdings%20I%2C%20Inc.%E2%80%99s%20right)

Twitter denied breaching the agreement or facing a Company Material Adverse Effect. It said the buyers had themselves materially breached the agreement and that their notice was invalid. The board said it would seek enforcement in the Delaware Court of Chancery, and Twitter reserved the right to seek specific performance to compel the buyers to close at USD 54.20 per share. The record showed a dispute, not a ruling: neither termination nor closing was established.
