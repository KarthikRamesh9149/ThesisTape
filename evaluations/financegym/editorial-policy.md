# Funded completion on the original sources

The first live question that exhausted both original investigators and had no independently approved draft concerned equity-market risk. Its primary researcher could not reserve its revision and the independent investigator received an incomplete forecasting response before its draft. This additional procedure was specified during the run. All original registered attempts still count in their respective release rates.

Only an otherwise unanswered original question qualifies. Verify both original ledgers, source searches, cutoff and attempts. Try the registered, zero-cost publication of independently approved paragraphs first. A complete or qualified answer from any preceding step closes the question. The editor receives the unchanged public question, cutoff and only verified passages from the original two investigations. It receives neither their proposed answers nor any grading material. Its editing process cannot fetch another document.

Give the editor a separate USD 0.55 ceiling. A Sol analyst proposes calculations, code checks their operands, an Astra author writes a short source-grounded memo, and a fresh Sol reviewer checks each paragraph against its passages. If no paragraph passes, the author may make one correction with funds reserved for independent review. Release only reviewed paragraphs after the ordinary citation, arithmetic and question-attribution checks. Label this output qualified. Preserve all prior failures, source editions, editing time, unique provider responses and expenses. These reports are an explicitly post-registration contribution to the full candidate.
