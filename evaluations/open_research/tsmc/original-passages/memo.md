# Investment research memo

As of 2024-04-10

**Research question**
As of 10 April 2024, what financing had the U.S. Department of Commerce proposed for TSMC Arizona, how did TSMC’s planned investment and third fab fit the proposal, and which conditions could change what TSMC actually received?

Some parts of the question could not be established from eligible evidence.

## Answer

As of 2024-04-10, Commerce and TSMC Arizona had signed nonbinding preliminary CHIPS Act terms proposing up to $6.6 billion in direct funding and approximately $5 billion in loans. Commerce had not made a final award. The proposal would support TSMC’s expanded Phoenix manufacturing plan: the company’s planned Arizona investment had grown from $40 billion to more than $65 billion. That larger figure was TSMC’s planned investment, not the federal award. [1](https://web.archive.org/web/20240410192324/https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2024/04/biden-harris-administration-announces-preliminary-terms-tsmc-expanded#:~:text=Media%20Contact%3A%20Madeline%20Broas%2C%20madeline.broas%20%5Bat%5D%20chips.gov%20%28madeline%5Bdot%5Dbroas%5Bat%5Dchips%5Bdot%5Dgov%29%20Today%2C,the%20most%20advanced%20process%20technology%20to%20the%20United%20States.) [2](https://web.archive.org/web/20240410192324/https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2024/04/biden-harris-administration-announces-preliminary-terms-tsmc-expanded#:~:text=In%20addition%20to%20the%20proposed%20direct%20funding%20of%20up,with%20additional%20PMT%20announcements%20expected%20to%20follow%20throughout%202024.) [3](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?oldid=1217253908)

The proposal covered the full three-fab project. TSMC committed to build a third leading-edge fab in Phoenix alongside the two previously announced; Commerce did not propose a separate grant for it. The announced commitment was to build the third fab before the end of the decade. The available announcement establishes that construction horizon, not a date for production to begin. [1](https://web.archive.org/web/20240410192324/https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2024/04/biden-harris-administration-announces-preliminary-terms-tsmc-expanded#:~:text=Media%20Contact%3A%20Madeline%20Broas%2C%20madeline.broas%20%5Bat%5D%20chips.gov%20%28madeline%5Bdot%5Dbroas%5Bat%5Dchips%5Bdot%5Dgov%29%20Today%2C,the%20most%20advanced%20process%20technology%20to%20the%20United%20States.)

Three different tests governed what TSMC could receive: Commerce’s due diligence and negotiations would determine the final award, project milestones would govern payments, and qualified spending would determine the separate Treasury investment tax credit. TSMC described the proposed loans as up to US$5 billion—available borrowing, not additional grant money. It planned to claim a credit of up to 25% of qualified capital expenditures; applying that rate to the entire announced investment would assume every dollar qualified. [2](https://web.archive.org/web/20240410192324/https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2024/04/biden-harris-administration-announces-preliminary-terms-tsmc-expanded#:~:text=In%20addition%20to%20the%20proposed%20direct%20funding%20of%20up,with%20additional%20PMT%20announcements%20expected%20to%20follow%20throughout%202024.) [4](https://web.archive.org/web/20240410192230/https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/3122#:~:text=TSMC%20practices%20green%20manufacturing%20and%20aims%20to%20serve%20as,are%20subject%20to%20regulatory%20approvals%20in%20Taiwan%20as%20necessary.)

## Outlook

The base case was a negotiated award followed by payments as work progressed, not an immediate transfer of the proposed maximum. The signed preliminary terms followed merit review, but the first confirming signal would be an executed award stating the amount and conditions. After that, the relevant signals would be milestone completion and the corresponding payments. The announcement did not specify TSMC’s milestones or installment sizes. [2](https://web.archive.org/web/20240410192324/https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2024/04/biden-harris-administration-announces-preliminary-terms-tsmc-expanded#:~:text=In%20addition%20to%20the%20proposed%20direct%20funding%20of%20up,with%20additional%20PMT%20announcements%20expected%20to%20follow%20throughout%202024.) [5](https://web.archive.org/web/20240404165501/https://www.nist.gov/chips/funding-updates#:~:text=Due%20diligence%3A%20If%20a%20full%20application%20appears%20reasonably%20likely,to%20be%20disbursed%20in%20tranches%20tied%20to%20project%20milestone.)
